ELC 275-501 capstone · self-leveling quad · PIC32MZ EF Starter Kit · J12 40-pin header

J12 universal pinout

The one wiring reference for the whole project: what goes on every J12 pin, and what each part needs. Version 1, Nov 16 2026. Pin choices are checked against Microchip's starter kit guide (DS70005230B, Table 2-3) and the PIC32MZ EF datasheet (DS60001320H, Tables 12-2 and 12-3).

This sheet is the rule. Every wire goes where this sheet says, at every step. If a pin ever has to change, change it here first (and add a line to the change log at the bottom), then in PINOUT_AND_PLAN.md, CLAUDE.md and the code, all at once.

The header

J12 (2 × 20, on the back of the starter kit)
Drawn with pin 1 at the top. Pin 1 has the square pad. Pin 2 is right across from pin 1, then 3-4, 5-6 and so on down the header.
Odd pins are one row, even pins the other. Turn the board so its pin 1 matches this drawing.

Power path

  • 3S LiPo (11.1 V, 12.6 V full) → XT60 → PDB. Solder a 470 µF 50 V capacitor across the PDB battery pads.
  • PDB → each ESC's thick red/black power leads → motors.
  • Flight: PDB 5 V BEC → micro-USB cable → kit J4. Never power the kit through the J12 +5 V pins.
  • Bench: the kit runs from the PC on J1. Don't plug the BEC into J4 while J1 is connected.
  • All grounds end up common through the ESC ground wires (pins 14/20/25/30).

Each LED needs a 330 Ω resistor: pin → 330 Ω → LED long leg; LED short leg → pin 34.
Buzzer: small 3.3 V buzzer: pin 35 → buzzer + ; buzzer − → pin 34. If it is too weak or a 5 V type: pin 35 → 1 kΩ → 2N3904 base; emitter → pin 34; collector → buzzer −; buzzer + → pin 4 (+5 V); 1N4148 across the buzzer, stripe to +.

Battery voltage sense STEP 6

  • PDB battery + → 33 kΩ → middle point → 10 kΩ → ground.
  • Middle point → J12 pin 15 (RB3 = AN3, analog).
  • 0.1 µF from pin 15 to ground (smooths the reading).
  • Pin 15 voltage = battery × 10 / 43: 12.6 V full → 2.93 V, 9.9 V empty → 2.30 V. Always under 3.3 V.

Rules that never change

  1. Pins 8 and 10 are the PC serial link (PuTTY and the PID tuner). Never wire them. Never press SW3.
  2. 3.3 V parts stay on 3.3 V (pins 1 and 17): IMU and VL53L0X. Never 5 V.
  3. Only pins 7, 11, 12, 15, 19, 21, 37 can carry PWM, SPI data or analog. They are all used. The ESCs can't move to other pins.
  4. Every ESC signal wire gets its own ground wire to the J12 ground next to it (14, 20, 25, 30). The ESC's red wire is never connected.
  5. SPI1 is shared: one CS per device (IMU 16, Bluefruit 13), only one CS low at a time.
  6. Pin 3 belongs to the VL53L0X I2C bus. The IMU's CS moved to pin 16 on Nov 15 for this reason.
  7. Props off until Step 4, the seesaw clamped, and SW1 (kill) within reach whenever the battery is plugged in.
  8. Changing a pin means changing this sheet, PINOUT_AND_PLAN.md, CLAUDE.md and the code together.
